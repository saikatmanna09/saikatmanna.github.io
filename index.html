👋 About Me

I’m not trying to be perfect. I’m simply trying to grow, create, learn, and leave a little piece of myself wherever I go. 🌎✨

I believe that every experience teaches us something, every mistake helps us grow, and every small step can take us closer to the person we want to become.

I’m not afraid of making mistakes, because I believe mistakes are a part of learning. What matters is having the courage to learn from them, keep moving forward, and become a little better than I was yesterday.

I have many dreams in my eyes, many ideas in my mind, and a long way to go. I enjoy my own company and find a quiet kind of peace in solitude. Sometimes, being alone gives me the space to think, create, dream, and simply be myself.

I’m still learning, still exploring, and still figuring things out — but I’m enjoying the journey along the way. 🚀

This is my little world, built from the things I love, the memories I carry, and the dreams I’m working towards.

And this is only the beginning… ✨

🌸 Maa

Maa ❤️

Forever in my heart.

There are some people we never truly lose, because they become a part of who we are.

My Maa was one of those people.

She was not just my Maa; she was my safest place, my comfort, and a very special part of my world. Some of my most precious memories are the little things she did for me—things that may have seemed ordinary at the time, but became priceless memories as the years passed.

I still remember the warmth of her care, the way she looked after me, and how she always wanted to see me happy. Even the smallest moments with her now feel like treasures that I carry quietly in my heart.

She left this world in 2017, but her place in my life never left with her.

There are days when I wish I could simply sit beside her again, talk to her about my life, and tell her about the person I am slowly becoming. I wish she could see my dreams, my struggles, my little achievements, and all the things I am still trying to accomplish.

I cannot bring those moments back, but I can carry her love forward.

Whatever I become in life, a part of it will always belong to the woman who gave me so much love before I even understood how precious it was.

You may no longer be here beside me, Maa, but you will always be a part of me.

I miss you.
I remember you.
And I will carry your love with me, always. ❤️

In loving memory 🕊️
